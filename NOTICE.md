The ability modifier, point-buy table, d20 selection and Training schedule were carried forward from the Sanguine Archipelago implementation of the adapted rules. This package uses Archipelago attribute and Training terminology and separates dice selection from host randomness. Tool aliases retain the existing grant and inventory IDs. The equipment Training functions accept host-supplied groups and profiles. The equipment tables retain their SRD 5.2 attribution in src/equipment/NOTICE.md. The Gunslinger Calling and Deadeye Path adapt the SRD Fighter and Champion. The Iron Suppressor, Brass Outrider and Cinder Artillerist Path material retains its Sanguine Archipelago source attribution.

The pool spending and Initiative recovery transitions carry forward existing Sanguine Archipelago mechanics. Their Calling and Path grants and IDs are supplied by the host.

The complete roster, Path grants and progression tables retain attribution in their feature records. Adapted Calling and Path material uses the same SRD 5.2.1 license above.
